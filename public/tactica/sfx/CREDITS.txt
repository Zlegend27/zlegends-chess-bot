Chess Tactica sound effects

All recorded sounds in this folder are from Kenney's free game asset packs
(www.kenney.nl), released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  Impact Sounds (1.0)     wood, plank, punch, plate, metal, soft, bell, glass impacts, wooden footstep
  RPG Audio               knife slices, knife draw, chop, cloth, coins, creak, leather drop, metal click
  Interface Sounds (1.0)  tick, error, confirmation, bong, maximize, scratch
  Sci-Fi Sounds (1.0)     large laser, force field, explosion crunch, low-frequency explosion

They were mixed to mono, resampled to 32 kHz and encoded as 64 kbps MP3.
The fly buzz is synthesized in code (src/tactica/sound.js), not a recording.
