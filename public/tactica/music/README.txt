Chess Tactica's music goes in this folder, as .mp3 files with these names.
A track that isn't here simply doesn't play. Only put music here that you
have the right to publish: this folder is part of the public site. All
from OpenGameArt.org, re-encoded to 128 kbps (loops made from the
composer's looped WAVs where there were some). Credited in Tactica >
Settings > Credits and in the end credits. Which floor plays which file:
FLOOR_MUSIC in src/tactica/worlds.js.
